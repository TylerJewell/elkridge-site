Wealth structuring

Wealth structured for life after the transaction.

Coordinated with your own attorney and CPA.

What the structures do

Growth, annual tax and protection, often together.

Drive growth

Cash that stays in the structure is reinvested in more companies, real estate and other holdings.

Optimize the annual tax burden

Ownership, depreciation, compensation and charitable giving are arranged so that less of each year's income goes to tax.

Protect your assets

Separate entities, trusts and insurance place distance between business risk and family wealth.

The vehicles

A full range of entities, trusts and insurance structures.

Asset-holding LLCs

Equipment and other business assets sit in a separate LLC leased to the operating company, so depreciation reduces taxable income and the assets stay outside the company's liabilities.

Tax · Protection

Real estate entities

Property used by the business is owned separately and leased to it, building rental income, depreciation and a second source of wealth.

Growth · Tax · Protection

Holding companies

A holding company owns the operating companies, collects their distributions and reinvests across them.

Growth · Protection

Management services organizations

An MSO supplies administration, staff and systems to operating companies under written agreements.

Growth · Tax

Business trusts

A trust holds the business interests and passes income to its beneficiaries.

Tax · Protection

Entity elections and compensation design

The tax status of each entity and the split of income among salary, distributions and service payments shape the tax each year.

Tax

Charitable trusts

A charitable remainder trust, for example, pays income for a term and then passes the remainder to charity, and it can defer the gain on a sale.

Growth · Tax

Charitable LLCs and family foundations

The family directs its giving through its own vehicle and keeps a say in how funds are invested and distributed.

Tax

Private insurance companies

A company owned by the family or the business insures the operating company's risks, with reserves that accumulate inside the family's structure.

Growth · Tax · Protection

Dynasty trusts

A trust holds assets for several generations, outside the estate and shielded from creditors.

Growth · Protection

Asset protection trusts

Assets move into a protective trust ahead of any claim.

Protection

Sale-proceeds strategies

Installment sales and exchange or reinvestment provisions spread or defer the gain on a sale.

Growth · Tax
